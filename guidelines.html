Official UPSC CSAT Rules & Test Instructions

  • Qualifying Nature: UPSC Civil Services Prelims Paper-II (CSAT) is qualifying. You must secure a minimum of 33% marks (8.25 marks out of 25 in this mock) to qualify.
  • Negative Marking: For each wrong answer, one-third (0.83 marks) of the marks assigned to that question will be deducted as penalty.
  • Unattempted Questions: If a question is left blank, i.e., no answer is marked by the candidate, there will be no penalty for that question.
  • Question Navigation: You can navigate between questions using the top number bar (1 to 10) or Next/Previous buttons. Questions can also be marked for review.
  • Timer Tracking: Live time spent on each individual question as well as total test time will be monitored to help identify time traps.

Note: You can also switch languages on-the-fly inside the test room.
